title: Introduction

---

Shotstash turns the PC, NAS or spare drive you already own into a media cloud built for work: projects and sections instead of a flat photo stream, a viewer that respects portrait video, resumable chunked uploads straight from the browser, share links you can hand to a client, and roles for the people you work with.

It is MIT licensed. There is no paid tier and nothing is held back: every feature described here is in the repository.

## What you get

- **Projects, sections and files**, with grid and list views, sort and search.
- **Uploads that survive bad Wi-Fi**: parallel parts with retries, resumable after a reload, checksum-verified, with duplicate detection per project.
- **Storage on your terms**: a local disk, a NAS mount or any S3-compatible bucket.
- **A viewer** for photos and video that follows the file's real aspect ratio.
- **Share links** per file, section or project, public or protected by an access code, with a clean client page and ZIP download.
- **Roles**: super admin, admin, crew, editor and viewer, with account approval.
- **An API**: GraphQL over HTTP and WebSocket, plus REST routes for uploads, downloads, sharing, the worker pipeline and health.
- **Bring your own AI**: a small job contract so any program that speaks HTTP can make proxies, transcripts or anything else.

## Status

Shotstash is pre-release. Databases created before version 1.0.0 may have to be started over on an upgrade; the [upgrade guide](/docs/upgrading/) says when. Follow the [changelog](/docs/changelog/) for releases.
