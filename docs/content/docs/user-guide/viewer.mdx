Click a file to open it in the viewer. Photos and videos keep their real shape: a vertical phone video is shown vertical, not cropped into a landscape box.

<Screenshot name="viewer" alt="The viewer showing a landscape image, with Share and Download buttons and arrows to the previous and next file" />

## Moving around

| Action | Keyboard | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next or previous file | Right and Left arrows | swipe, or the arrows |
| Play or pause a video | Space | tap the video |
| Mute | M | the speaker button |
| Full screen | F | the full screen button |
| Close | Esc | the close button |

## File details

The **info** button opens the details panel: type, size, dimensions and orientation, duration for videos, when it was uploaded, and the project and section it belongs to. On a phone the panel slides up from the bottom; **Show all info** expands it.

The panel also lists the file's **versions**, such as a 720p proxy made by a [processing job](/docs/user-guide/jobs/). Play a version in the viewer or download it.

## Downloading and sharing

**Download** saves the original file, exactly as it was uploaded. **Share** creates a link to this file for people outside your team; see [Sharing](/docs/user-guide/sharing/). To download several files at once, select them in the section and choose **Download ZIP**.
