This guide is for everyone who works in Shotstash day to day: uploading footage, finding it again, reviewing it and handing it to a client. Setting up the server is covered in the [Quick start](/docs/quick-start/).

<Screenshot name="projects" height={1060} alt="The Projects page with four project folders, each showing previews of its files" />

## The idea in one paragraph

Work is organised in **projects** (a campaign, a documentary, a shoot). A project has **sections** (Day 1 footage, Selects, Stills, Deliverables) and sections hold **files**. You upload into a section, open files in the **viewer**, talk about them in the project's **discussion**, and send a **share link** to people outside your team. What you may do depends on your **role**.

## Signing in

Open the address your administrator gave you and sign in with your email and password (or Google, when your installation offers it). A new account may wait for approval first; you see a waiting screen until an admin approves it. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page when your installation sends email.
