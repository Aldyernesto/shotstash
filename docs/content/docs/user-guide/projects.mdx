## Projects

**My Media** lists every project as a folder with a peek at its latest files. Hover over a project (or tap it on a phone) to look inside, click it to open it. **New Project** creates one with a title and an optional description.

## Sections

Inside a project, files live in **sections**. A new project starts with a few sections you can rename or remove; **New Section** adds more. A section card shows a preview and its file count; an empty section offers **Upload first footage** and accepts files dragged onto it.

<Screenshot name="project" alt="The Harbor Lights campaign project with its sections: Deliverables, Stills, Selects, Day 1 footage and more" />

Open a section to see its files. Switch between the **grid** and the **list** with the buttons next to the search field, and sort by name, date, size or type.

<Screenshot name="section" alt="The Stills section in grid view: eight landscape images with their names and sizes" />

## Finding things

The search field finds files and sections by name. Results update as you type.

## Moving, renaming and deleting

Every file and section has an actions menu (the three dots): **Rename**, **Move to…** another section or project, share, and **Move to Trash**. You can also drag files onto another section to move them. **Select** lets you pick several files and download them as one ZIP or move them to the Trash together.

Trashed items wait in the **Trash** and can be restored. They are deleted for good after 30 days (your administrator can change this), and share links to them stop working at once.
