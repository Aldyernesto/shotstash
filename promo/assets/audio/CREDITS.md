# Audio credits

Audio for the 30 second Shotstash launch trailer. Collected 7 Oct 2026.

Only files marked **Committable: yes** (CC0) may enter git. Every other file is
listed in `.gitignore`, stays on the local machine, and only reaches the public
through the mixed MP4. Their licenses allow use inside a video but forbid
redistributing the raw file.

Licenses used:

- **CC0 1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Pixabay Content License**: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
  (use in a video is fine, no attribution needed, the raw file may not be
  redistributed on its own)
- **Mixkit Sound Effects Free License**: https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree
  (use in a video is fine, no attribution needed, "You can't redistribute the
  Item on its own, as stock, in a tool or template, or with source files")

BPM values are estimates from onset autocorrelation and hit spacing (ffmpeg
analysis); none of the source pages lists a BPM.

## Main music cut (bgm-main.mp3)

Two segments, one splice, total exactly 30.00 s:

1. Source 13.00 s to 34.60 s, placed at timeline 0.00 s (fade in 0.4 s).
2. Source 73.03 s to 81.43 s, placed at timeline 21.60 s (40 ms crossfade),
   fade out from 29.0 s to 30.0 s.

Reference ffmpeg command:

```
ffmpeg -i bgm-main.mp3 -filter_complex "[0]atrim=13.00:34.64,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,afade=t=in:d=0.4[a];[0]atrim=73.03:81.43,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS[b];[a][b]acrossfade=d=0.04:c1=tri:c2=tri,afade=t=out:st=29.0:d=1.0[o]" -map "[o]" bgm-cut.wav
```

Measured on that render: -12.5 LUFS integrated, 9.0 LU loudness range.
